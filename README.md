## 🎨 About Me

I’m a perfectionist and a graphic designer.

I play [Minecraft](https://namemc.com/profile/gubinmc.1) - though it’s more accurate to say I create server modpacks than actually play the game.  
I’ve been in Minecraft for about 8-9 years. I have solid experience in creating, optimizing, and configuring modpacks. If you need help with this, just reach out.
I enjoy sites like modrinth and black-minecraft, where you’ll often find me.  
I'm the owner of the ExultantHarmony project ([exult.fun](https://exult.fun/)). But since I’m working on it alone, development is going pretty slowly.

---

## 🖌️ Design

I’m proficient with Adobe Photoshop, Figma, Blender, and Blockbench.  
Modeling in Blender isn’t my strong suit - mostly, I use it to render Minecraft skins. I want to learn how to create textures and models in Blockbench, and eventually to draw Minecraft skins myself.
My design work is strongly tied to Minecraft. I make covers, avatars, and banners with your Minecraft skins, and each time I get better and better.
While Minecraft is often my theme of choice, I'm happy to design for other subjects too!  
I have a portfolio website where I showcase my works: [gubinmc.vercel.app](https://gubinmc.vercel.app/)
